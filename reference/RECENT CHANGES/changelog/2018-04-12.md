- Public release of version** 2.0**
- [Institution](https://basiq.readme.io/v2.0/reference#institutions) resource is changed - this is a breaking change.
- Merchant details are removed from [Transaction](https://basiq.readme.io/v2.0/reference#transactions) resource - this is a breaking change.
- Added filters to [Institutions](https://basiq.readme.io/v2.0/reference#institutions) collection. Supported filters include filtering by country, tier, serviceType and institutionType properties. At the moment, these filters support equality operation.
- Added tier property to [Institution](https://basiq.readme.io/v2.0/reference#institutions) resource. This property reflects the overall impact of the Institution, it's business and market share in the relevant country/region. Values range from 1 to 4, with 1 marking the highest impact on the market. For example, CBA is a Tier 1 Bank in Australia, Suncorp is Tier 2, etc.
