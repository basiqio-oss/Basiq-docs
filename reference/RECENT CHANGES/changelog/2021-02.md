* Added external payment providers and liabilities to [Affordability API](ref:affordability-api)  to surface relationships that are held externally to the bank accounts that have not been disclosed or linked by the user, e.g. surfacing small pay-day loans or buy now pay later services. These are now represented as external payments rather than expenses. First version of this feature includes the most commonly requested of these merchants:
  * AfterPay
  * Cash Direct
  * Cash Converters
  * Open Pay
  * Latitude Loans
  * Sunshine Loans
  * Zip Pay/Zip Money
  * Cash Stop
  * Gem Visa
  * Q Card

* Added new sandbox user - login Whistler [Testing](ref:testing) to represent another type of persona with different spending behaviour such as  BNPL activity and Affordability data such as irregular income.

* Added support added for 3 more banks to support parsing of PDF bank [statements](ref:create-a-statement)   for account types: `savings`, `transaction`, `credit-card`, `mortgage`
  * AU00301 Macquarie
  * AU00401 BankWest
  * AU00101 Bendigo

* Added multiple new [Institutions](ref:institutions) connectors including:
  * AU16101 Latitude GO - beta
  * AU16301 Latitude 28 Degrees - beta
  * AU16001 Virgin - beta
  * AU16201 Latitude Low Rate - beta
