- AU00301 (Macquarie)  - update to provide a correct value for accounts.accountNo attribute for Cash Management Accounts. Macquarie report a 12-digit account number for their Cash Management Accounts - three leading zeroes followed by a 9-digit account number.  Basiq is now stripping the leading zeroes and returning the 6-digit BSB followed by the 9-digit account number - without leading zeroes.
