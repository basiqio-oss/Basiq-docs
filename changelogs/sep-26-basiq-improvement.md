We’ve made a couple of improvements this month to make working with Basiq more reliable and expand the institutions available to connect to.

## 📄 More reliable PDF reports

Large transaction reports are now handled more reliably. If a PDF cannot be generated due to the size of the report, partners can still retrieve the underlying report data instead of the report generation failing.

## 🏦 New institution: Revolut

Revolut is now available as a supported Australian financial institution in Basiq.

If you have any questions about these updates, please reach out to Basiq Support.
