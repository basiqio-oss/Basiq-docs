We're excited to announce some key changes aimed at enhancing the experience for our partners on Basiq Dashboard and Basiq ConsentUI. Here's a rundown of what's new:

## Business Consumer Changes :briefcase:

We've rolled out [Business Consumer](https://api.basiq.io/docs/business-consumer-consent) changes to Basiq Dashboard and Basiq ConsentUI.

* **Business Applications:** Partners can now seamlessly create Business applications directly through Basiq Dashboard.
* **Creation of Business Users:** Partners now have the capability to [create Business users](https://api.basiq.io/reference/createuser) complete with Business-specific fields.
* **Enhanced User Interface:** Business details are now conveniently accessible in the side menu of the User screen in Basiq Dashboard.
* **Expanded Features for Business Users:** Business-type users can now access additional fields within the ConsentUI.
* **Open Banking Scopes:** Business-type users can easily create and share business scopes via Open Banking.

## Customiser Enhancement

* **Custom Brand Name:** Partners can now configure the name displayed to their customers in the Customiser. [Which is now a required field](https://api.basiq.io/docs/basiq-customise-ui#quick-links).
* **Mandatory Brand Name:** We've made Brand name a mandatory value for all applications, including non-Business Consumer Changes (BCC). Partners who haven't defined it yet will be prompted to do so the next time they edit ConsentUI.

**Removal of Payment Application Option:** With this update, we've removed the option for partners to create Payment applications.

If you have any questions or need further assistance, don't hesitate to reach out to our support team.
