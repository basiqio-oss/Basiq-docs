We've expanded Basiq's CDR connector coverage with seven new Non-Bank Lender (NBL) connectors.

The new connectors cover the following automotive finance brands:

## What's new

Partners can now select and test the following institutions through Basiq:

- Toyota Finance (AU21060)
- Lexus Financial Services (AU21070)
- Hino Financial Services (AU21080)
- PowerTorque Finance (AU21090)
- Mazda Finance (AU21100)
- Power Alliance Finance (AU21110)
- Suzuki Financial Services (AU21120)

## Getting started

Partners can find these institutions in the institution selection flow and try connecting their accounts through the existing Basiq integration.

For questions or assistance with testing, please contact Basiq Support.
