We've expanded Basiq's CDR connector coverage with **seven new Non-Bank Lender (NBL) connectors** for automotive finance brands.

## What's new

Partners can now select and test the following institutions through Basiq:

<Columns layout="auto">
  <Column>

- **Toyota Finance** — `AU21060`
- **Lexus Financial Services** — `AU21070`
- **Hino Financial Services** — `AU21080`
- **PowerTorque Finance** — `AU21090`

  </Column>
  <Column>

- **Mazda Finance** — `AU21100`
- **Power Alliance Finance** — `AU21110`
- **Suzuki Financial Services** — `AU21120`

  </Column>
</Columns>

Find these institutions in the institution selection flow and try connecting your accounts through your existing Basiq integration.
