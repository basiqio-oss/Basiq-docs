We are excited to share the latest updates and improvements we've made to the API and Dashboard in September 2024. These updates focus on enhancing user experience, improving connectivity, and providing more accurate data insights. Here's what's new:

***

## 🌟 **New Event Types for Partners**

Partners can now subscribe to three new event types:  

* `connection.invalidated`  
* `account.updated`  
* `transactions.updated`  

[Learn more about event types](https://api.basiq.io/reference/listeventtypes).

***

## 🔗 **New CDR Connectors Added**

We are thrilled to announce the addition of new connectors:  

* **Liberty Financial**  
* **Northern Inland Credit Union Limited**  
* **Great Southern Bank Business+**  

These connectors are live and available in the `GET /connectors` and `GET /institutions` endpoints, supporting **Open Banking** and **CSV** methods.  

[More about CDR Connectors](https://api.basiq.io/docs/connecting-business-accounts-via-cdr).

***

## 🚀 **Income Verification Tutorial**

We are excited to introduce our **Income Verification Tutorial**, designed to help users automate the process of verifying their income. This tutorial provides guidance on securely connecting bank accounts and generating comprehensive income reports using the Reports API.  

Explore the [Income Verification Tutorial](https://api.basiq.io/docs/income-verification-iav) and [Reports API](https://api.basiq.io/docs/reporting) to learn more..
