# Basiq Dashboard :heart_decoration:

***

We are thrilled to announce a series of substantial improvements to the Dashboard Reports feature, aiming to enhance user interactions and optimise the overall user experience. 

## **Print and Export Reports to PDF** :page_facing_up:

You can now easily [print](https://dashboard.basiq.io/reports/all) or export reports to PDF directly from the Basiq Dashboard. Simply click on the  `Print` button on the top right to print. Please refer to below image.

## **Average Monthly Credits/Debits** :money_with_wings:

We've added a new feature on the Summary page that displays average monthly credits and debits, providing you with more insightful financial data.

## **Customisable Request Consent Actions:** :e-mail:

On the Request Consent page, partners can now specify custom action methods such as "connect", "manage", "extend," and "update".

## **Improved Permission Sets:** :mag:

We've revamped the grouping of [permission sets for Events and Webhooks](https://dashboard.basiq.io/settings/permissionSets), ensuring a more organised and intuitive experience when managing permissions.

## **Enhanced Phone Number Validation:** :iphone:

Our phone number validation system has been updated to support new formats, making it easier to input and verify phone numbers accurately.

## **Updated Favicon:** :earth_africa:

We've given the Basiq Dashboard a fresh look with an updated favicon.

## **Transaction Sorting:** :tada:

Transactions on the Dashboard are now ordered correctly, providing a more seamless and logical viewing experience.

## **New Metrics ME042 and ME043:** :new:

Two new metrics, [ME042 and ME043](https://api.basiq.io/reference/get_reports-id), have been added for income. 

## **Basiq Connect Enhancement:** :iphone:

The [Basiq Connect feature](https://dashboard.basiq.io), along with the associated NPM package, now supports the same action parameters as ConsentUI, aligning the functionalities and providing a more seamless experience for users.
